📋 What Data is Tracked Across the 58,882 Visits in anc_history?
58,882 Clinical Records Analyzed

Each visit record in anc_history represents a complete medical consultation and sonography assessment. The table contains rich multidimensional clinical data grouped into 6 primary medical domains:

1. Ultrasound & Fetal Biometry
  • Gestational Sac & Yolk Sac: Sac diameter (oe_ut_sac), yolk sac visibility & dimensions.
  • Viability & Cardiac Pulsations: Fetal cardiac activity (fcp present/absent) & early fetal pole.
  • Crown-Rump Length (CRL): Direct mm biometry (crl) for exact first-trimester dating.
  • Amniotic Fluid & Liquor: Liquor index (liquor_type: Normal, Oligohydramnios, Polyhydramnios).
  • Placenta & Position: Anterior, posterior, fundal, low-lying placenta & fetal presentation (cephalic/vertex/breech).
  • Doppler & Cervical Length: Uterine artery Doppler and cervical incompetence checks.
2. Laboratory & Diagnostic Panels
  • Hematology & Anemia: Baseline & late-term Hemoglobin (investigation_early_scan_hb, growth_hb).
  • Thyroid Function: Serum TSH monitoring (investigation_tsh) for fetal brain development.
  • Gestational Diabetes Panels: Fasting (FBS), Post-Prandial (PP2BS), and Random (RBS) blood sugars.
  • Infectious Serology: Mandatory screening for HIV 1&2, HBsAg (Hep B), HCV, and VDRL (Syphilis).
  • Blood Typing & Urine: ABO Rh blood grouping and routine microscopy for proteinuria/infection.
3. Prescribed Antenatal Medications
  • Progesterone Injections: High-dose INJ SUSTAIN 100MG, STRONE 100, and INJ PRO-9.
  • Oral/Vaginal Micronized Progesterone: CAP GESTUS-400, CAP GUFIPRO-400, and EMPROGEST GEL 0.8%.
  • Microcirculation Support: Ecosprin (low-dose aspirin for preeclampsia prophylaxis).
  • Hormonal Support: INJ HCG 5000 / 10,000 IU for early pregnancy luteal maintenance.
  • Vitamins & Anti-Emetics: Folic acid, Calcium (CAROL CALOM CORAL), and TAB DOXIMOM-OD.
4. Vaccinations & Lung Maturity
  • Tetanus Toxoid: First dose (tt1) and booster (tt2) logging.
  • Fetal Lung Maturity: Betamethasone / Dexamethasone doses (betnasol_1, betnasol_2) in preterm risks.
  • Iron & Vitamin Infusions: Intravenous iron sucrose for severe gestational anemia.
5. Milestone Scans & Timelines
  • Early Viability Scan: Conducted at 6–8 weeks (early_scan).
  • NT Scan (Nuchal Translucency): 11–13.6 weeks for Down Syndrome & chromosomal screening (nt_scan).
  • Anomaly Scan / TIFFA: 18–22 weeks structural defect evaluation (anomalies_miles).
  • Growth & Doppler Scan: 28–36 weeks fetal weight trajectory and placental blood supply (growth_scan).
6. Term Delivery & Birth Outcomes
  • Delivery Method: Lower Segment Cesarean Section (LSCS) vs Full-Term Normal Delivery (FTND).
  • Gestational Age at Birth: Full term delivery documented between 37 and 40 weeks.
  • Birth Weight & Fetal Sex: Estimated and recorded birth weight, singleton vs twin outcomes.
  • Total Live Births: 2,418 deliveries confirmed from fertility conversions.

4 Core Clinical Decision Dimensions Evaluated for Treatment Selection:
🥚 1. Ovarian Reserve (Antral Follicle Count - AFC)
High AFC (15-30+): Polycystic reserve indicates Letrozole to prevent OHSS & multiple births.
Normal (8-14): Clomiphene Citrate or Timed Coitus.
Low (<6): High-dose HMG (150 IU) or transition to IVF.
📅 2. Cycle Presentation Day (LMP Timing)
Day 2-5 (Early Follicular): Optimal window to initiate ovarian stimulation medications.
Day 12-14 (Mid-Cycle): Spontaneous dominant follicle already mature; assigned Natural Cycle Tracking.
🧬 3. Ovarian Morphology (PCOS Stroma)
Increased Stroma (PCOS): Letrozole is first-line aromatase inhibitor for monofollicular growth.
Normal Stroma: Standard CC or mild induction protocols.
🔬 4. Male Factor & Treatment History
Normal Semen: Mild oral agent with timed natural coitus.
Borderline / Oligo / Astheno: Sperm wash with Intrauterine Insemination (IUI).
Oral Failure: Adding injectable HMG gonadotropins.

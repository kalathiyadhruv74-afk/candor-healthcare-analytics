4 Core Clinical Decision Dimensions Evaluated for IVF Pathway Selection:
🎯 1. Cycle Stage & Clinical Visit Progression
Cycle 1 (Stimulation Visits): Controlled stimulation phase progressing toward Pick Up (Oocyte Retrieval).
Cycle 2+ (Transfer Visits): Retrieval completed; candidate proceeds to Frozen Embryo Transfer (FET).
🔬 2. Ovarian Reserve (AFC & AMH Status)
Active Reserve (8-16+): Ovarian capacity allows stimulation with patient's own eggs.
Depleted Reserve (<3 / POI): Diminished ovarian reserve or advanced age indicates Donor Oocyte (FET-OD).
🧬 3. Gamete Source & Embryo Provenance
Autologous Gametes: Normal semen and responsive follicles.
Dual Factor Infertility: Azoospermia combined with ovarian exhaustion indicates Donor Embryo (FET-ED).
🛡️ 4. Endometrial Priming & Synchronization
Hormone Replacement Protocol: Bypasses hyperstimulation hormonal stress; prepares receptive uterine lining via estrogen/progesterone priming.
